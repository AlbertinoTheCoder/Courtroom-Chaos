# Authored case library

All three cases use the same courthouse staff and vendor client, with fixed facts and authored dialogue. Evidence unlocks as testimony exposes missing records. Each has three contradictions, six exhibits, a closing choice and a complete verdict.

| Case | First contradiction | Second contradiction | Final proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Stolen Golden Gavel | Nozzle and red M identify mustard | Maintenance cleared the bench before arrival | Storage photograph and transfer record locate the gavel |
| The Blackout Banquet | Signed check shows battery power and an empty socket | Breaker timestamp contradicts the alleged arrival-time failure | Electrician reproduces a 2,700 W load on a 2,300 W circuit |
| The Charity Envelope | 16:35 photograph contradicts a continuously empty box | K4 opens a cupboard; D2 opens the donation box | Receipt N204 and the bank count track sealed C17 and all 480 pounds |

The new cases have their own evidence plates, posters and physical exhibits. The photograph shows the donation envelope inside its transparent collection box. The recovered envelope becomes inspectable only after its bank record enters evidence. The hot plate has an integrated battery cassette and no mains cable.

Three lives are shared by evidence and closing mistakes. The third error persists as a failed attempt; neither Continue nor reopening that case restores lives. Restart clears the complete attempt. Pressing, reviewing and inspecting do not consume lives. Per-case slots prevent one case from overwriting another. Version-2 five-confidence saves load with at most three lives and keep their trial progress.

The full cast shares the corrected mouth morphs and materials across all cases. Portrait fills make Brick and Victor readable in their speaking cameras. All 120 animation clips are loaded before play to avoid first-use pauses.

The banquet case now features Renzo Roux and Pip Sparks; the charity case features Nora Notes and Agnes Penny. Case CastModels map the existing client/witness gameplay roles to separate meshes. Client meshes use the Benny skeleton; witness meshes use the Milo skeleton. SpeakerNames and SpeakerRoles provide the corresponding presentation names without changing save identifiers.
