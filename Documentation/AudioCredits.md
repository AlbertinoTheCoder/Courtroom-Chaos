# Audio sources and regeneration

Character speech is synthesized with [Kokoro-82M v1.0](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), an Apache-2.0 model, through the MIT-licensed [kokoro-onnx runner](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx). Model weights and build dependencies remain under ignored Saved/TTS and are not distributed in the game. Only generated speech is cooked.

Oliver: bm_george; Victor: bm_fable; Bumble: am_santa; Benny: am_fenrir; Milo: am_puck; Brick: am_adam. Speed and pronunciation choices are recorded in Tools/build_voices.py and Art/VoiceManifest.json. These are synthetic voices, not recordings of actors.

Run Tools/BuildVoices.ps1 after editing dialogue and compiling the project. Cached unchanged clips are reused. The import builds /Game/Audio/DA_CourtAudio with exact dialogue keys, split-speaker feedback sequences, and 40 Hz amplitude envelopes for mouth animation. New or changed case text must be rebaked before packaging.

Music and foley are original procedural audio generated by Tools/build_audio.py and Tools/build_adaptive_music.py. The four music loops share 125 BPM, eight bars, and 15.36-second duration. Runtime keeps them playing together and crossfades their gains according to court temper. Menus lower intensity; speech and objections duck the mix. Settings provide independent voice and music controls.
