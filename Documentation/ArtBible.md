# Courtroom Chaos

Warm oak, cream plaster, oxblood carpet and brass establish the room. Teal, blue and purple separate the cast. Rounded profiles, thick furniture lips, inset panels and generous facial features carry the cartoon style. Materials use broad highlights rather than photoreal textures.

Characters use large heads, compact legs, oversized hands and layered costumes. Judge Bumble is round and heavy; Victor has broad shoulders and tall hair; Milo is narrow; Brick has a tiny head. Warm overhead keys and cool gallery fill separate faces from the architecture.

The interface uses ink panels, cream typography, gold rules and teal feedback. Keep trial controls in the lower frame, portraits above. Establishing, testimony and verdict shots use motivated cuts. Reactions should anticipate, snap and settle. Current motion uses rigid-segment skeletal rigs with head, arm, forearm, hand, leg, eyelid, brow and mouth controls. Seven baseline performance clips are selected by trial state. More expressive acting and precise hand/prop choreography remain later milestones.
